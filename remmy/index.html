Good to know

Does my audio leave my phone?
Never. Recordings, transcripts, and everything Remmy detects stay on your device. Sleep-talk is transcribed on-device — nothing is uploaded.
Do I need to keep the app open?
Yes — plug your phone in and leave Remmy open on your nightstand. It's built to listen through the night.
Is this a sleep-tracker or a medical tool?
Neither. Remmy is for fun and curiosity. It's not a medical device and doesn't detect sleep apnea or any health condition.
What does it cost?
A 3-day free trial, then $6.99/week or $39.99/year. Cancel anytime in your Apple ID settings.
