Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 16, 2026

Remmy records audio only while you're asleep and you've started a session — and everything it hears (the audio, the transcripts, the sounds it detects) stays on your device. Recordings are never uploaded to us or anyone else. Transcription happens entirely on your iPhone. There are no accounts and no cloud sync.

Overview

Remmy ("the App") is a sleep companion that listens while you sleep and surfaces the funny, surprising, and shareable things you say and the sounds you make — sleep-talk, snoring, and unexpected noises. Privacy is foundational to how it's built: the App is designed so that your recordings and everything derived from them never leave your device.

What the App Records

When you start a sleep session, Remmy uses your microphone to listen for sound. When it detects talking, snoring, or other noises, it saves short audio clips of those moments, along with an on-device transcript of any speech. This audio and its contents are:

  • Captured only during a sleep session you start
  • Processed and stored entirely on your iPhone
  • Never transmitted to our servers
  • Never synced to the cloud or backed up to any service of ours
  • Yours to play back, share, or delete at any time from within the App

On-Device Transcription

Remmy transcribes speech using Apple's on-device speech recognition. The App requires on-device recognition; if a device cannot perform speech recognition locally, Remmy simply skips transcription rather than sending any audio to Apple's servers. Your audio is not uploaded for transcription.

On-Device Sound Detection

Detection of snoring and other sounds is performed on your device using Apple's Sound Analysis framework. No audio is sent anywhere for this analysis.

No Accounts

Remmy does not require or offer account creation. There is no sign-up, no login, no email collection, and no user profile.

Microphone & Speech Permissions

The App asks for two iOS permissions so it can do its job:

  • Microphone — so Remmy can listen while you sleep. It only records during an active sleep session.
  • Speech Recognition — so Remmy can transcribe sleep-talk on your device.

You can revoke either permission at any time in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security.

Notifications

If you enable the optional bedtime reminder, the App schedules a local notification on your device. This is handled entirely by iOS; no notification data is sent to us.

No Cloud Sync

Your recordings, transcripts, and sleep history are stored exclusively on your device. There is no cloud sync, no backup service, and no remote storage of any kind. When you share a clip or a card, that sharing happens through iOS's standard share sheet at your choice, to the destination you pick.

No Third-Party Analytics or Ads in the App

The App does not include third-party analytics or advertising SDKs, and it does not build advertising profiles or sell data. Your recordings and their contents are never shared with third parties.

Note: this website uses Google Analytics for basic page-visit statistics. That is separate from the App and involves no recording data.

Third-Party Services

Within the App, we rely only on Apple frameworks:

  • Speech (Apple) — on-device transcription of sleep-talk
  • Sound Analysis (Apple) — on-device detection of snoring and sounds
  • StoreKit (Apple) — subscriptions and purchases (Apple handles payment)

In-App Purchases

Remmy is a paid subscription app. Purchases are processed by Apple through StoreKit. We do not receive or store your payment information. See our Terms of Service for subscription details.

Data Retention

Your recordings and sleep history remain on your device until you choose to delete them. You can:

  • Delete individual nights or clips within the App
  • Delete everything by removing the App from your device

Because we never receive this data, there is nothing for us to retain or delete on our end.

Not a Medical Service

Remmy is an entertainment and personal-curiosity app. It does not provide medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment, and it is not a medical device. It does not detect, diagnose, or monitor any medical condition, including sleep disorders. Do not rely on Remmy for health purposes; consult a qualified professional for any health concern.

Children's Privacy

The App is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will post any changes on this page and update the "Last updated" date above.
