Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 24, 2026

1. Data Collection and Storage

ScreenTax is built from the ground up to respect your privacy. We collect NO personal data. All data related to your screen time, app usage, and habits is stored locally on your device.

2. Apple FamilyControls and Screen Time API

To function as a self-discipline tool, ScreenTax utilizes Apple's native FamilyControls and Screen Time APIs. We request access to these features strictly to help you manage your own screen time. We cannot and do not transmit this data to our servers or any third parties.

3. Analytics and Tracking

We do not use any analytics, trackers, or third-party advertising SDKs. What you do on your device stays on your device.

4. In-App Purchases

All in-app purchases are handled entirely and securely through Apple's StoreKit API. Best Day Labs never collects, processes, or has access to your payment information.

5. Contact Us

ScreenTax is developed by Best Day Labs. If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at: support@bestdaylabs.com.
