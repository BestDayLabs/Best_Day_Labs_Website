Privacy Policy

Last Updated: March 2026

At Best Day Labs ("we", "our", or "us"), we are committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you use the Touge application.

1. Data We Collect

  • Account Information: Email address, username, vehicle information, and profile photos.
  • Driving Data: GPS coordinates, speed, distance, g-force metrics, and route paths.
  • Device Information: Device model, operating system, and unique device identifiers for analytics and troubleshooting.

2. How We Use Your Data

We use the collected information to:

  • Provide, maintain, and improve the Touge service.
  • Populate leaderboards and enable social features.
  • Process analytics to understand how our App is used.
  • Communicate with you regarding updates, security alerts, and support messages.

3. Third-Party Services

We utilize trusted third-party services to operate Touge. These include:

  • Supabase: Used for secure cloud database storage and user authentication.
  • Apple: Used for "Sign in with Apple" and processing in-app subscription payments.
  • LiveKit / Agora: Used to facilitate real-time voice chat features within squads.

4. Location Data Processing

Location data is central to Touge's functionality. It is only collected during active, user-initiated drives. Background location may be utilized to maintain tracking during a drive. Real-time location is only shared with other users when you explicitly enable "Squad Location Sharing."

5. Data Storage & Security

Your data is stored securely in the cloud via Supabase and locally on your device using SwiftData. We implement industry-standard security measures, including encryption in transit, to protect your personal information from unauthorized access.

6. Data Retention

Account data is retained as long as your account is active or until you request deletion. Driving data is kept on our servers to power your history and leaderboards until you explicitly delete the drive or your account.

7. Your User Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access and request an export of your personal data.
  • Request the deletion of specific drives or your entire account.
  • Opt-out of certain data collection features (though this may limit App functionality).

8. California Residents (CCPA)

If you are a California resident, you are granted specific rights under the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA), including the right to know what personal information is collected, the right to delete that information, and the right to opt-out of the sale of your personal information (Note: We do not sell your personal data).

9. Children's Privacy

Touge is not intended for use by children under the age of 13, and we are compliant with COPPA (Children’s Online Privacy Protection Act). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy periodically. We will notify you of any material changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page and updating the "Last Updated" date.

11. Contact Us

If you have any questions or concerns about this Privacy Policy or your data, please contact us at: support@bestdaylabs.com.
