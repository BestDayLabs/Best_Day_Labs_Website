Frequently Asked Questions

How does Riff work?

Riff uses AI to analyze audio and detect chords, beats, and lyrics. Import a song by sharing a link from YouTube, Spotify, or other sources. The audio is sent to our servers for analysis, and the results are displayed in real time as you play the song.

What sources can I import from?

Riff supports YouTube, Spotify, Apple Music, SoundCloud, TikTok, Instagram, and most other platforms that host audio or video content. You can also import audio files directly from your device.

How do I edit chords?

Tap any chord in the playback view to edit it. You can rename chords, adjust their timing, delete incorrect chords, or add missing ones. Your edits are saved locally to your library.

What is the catalog?

The catalog is a community-driven collection of chord analyses. You can publish your analyzed songs so other users can find them, and you can browse and save songs that others have published.

Is an account required?

No. You can analyze songs and use all core features without signing in. An account (via Sign-In with Apple) is only needed to publish to the catalog or vote on chord contributions.

I found a bug. How do I report it?

Please email us at the address above with a description of the bug, what you were doing when it happened, and your device model and iOS version.
