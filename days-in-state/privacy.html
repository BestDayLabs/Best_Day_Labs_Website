Privacy Policy

Last updated: January 24, 2026

🔒 TL;DR: Your location data stays on your device and in your private iCloud. We never see it, store it, or have access to it.

Overview

Days in State ("the App") is designed with privacy as a core principle. We believe your location data is sensitive and personal, and we've architected the App so that we never have access to it.

Data We Collect

We do not collect any personal data.

The App processes location data entirely on your device. This data is:

  • Stored locally on your iPhone
  • Optionally backed up to your private iCloud account (if you enable iCloud backup)
  • Never transmitted to our servers
  • Never shared with third parties

Location Data

The App uses your device's GPS to determine which state or country you are in. This location data:

  • Is processed entirely on your device
  • Is stored only on your device and/or your iCloud account
  • Is never sent to us or any third party
  • Can be deleted at any time from within the App

We use Apple's reverse geocoding service (which runs on your device) to convert GPS coordinates into state/country names. This is a standard iOS feature and does not transmit your precise location to us.

iCloud Backup

If you enable iCloud backup, your presence data is stored in your private iCloud database. This means:

  • The data is encrypted and stored in YOUR iCloud account
  • Only you can access it
  • We have no ability to read, access, or decrypt this data
  • Apple's iCloud security protections apply
  • You can restore your data if you get a new iPhone or reinstall the app

Analytics

We do not use any analytics services. We do not track:

  • How you use the App
  • What features you access
  • How often you open the App
  • Any other usage patterns

Third-Party Services

The App uses the following Apple services:

  • Core Location - For GPS-based state detection (on-device)
  • CloudKit - For optional iCloud backup (your private database)
  • StoreKit - For in-app purchases (Apple handles payment)

We do not use any third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking services.

In-App Purchases

Purchases are processed by Apple. We do not receive or store any payment information. Apple may share limited transaction data with us (such as whether a purchase was successful), but no personal financial information.

Data Retention

Since we don't collect your data, there's nothing to retain. Your data remains on your device and in your iCloud account until you choose to delete it.

You can delete all your data at any time:

  • Within the App via Settings → Delete All Data
  • By deleting the App from your device
  • By disabling iCloud backup and deleting iCloud data

Children's Privacy

The App is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect any information from children since we don't collect information from anyone.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page and updating the "Last updated" date.
