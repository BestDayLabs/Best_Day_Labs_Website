Privacy Policy

Last updated: December 28, 2025

Days in State ("we," "our," or "the App") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you use our iOS application.

Our Core Privacy Principle: Your location data never leaves your device. Days in State is designed from the ground up to keep all sensitive data entirely on your iPhone. We do not operate servers that collect or store your personal information.

1. Information We Collect

1.1 Location Data

Days in State collects location data to determine which US state you are currently in. This includes:

  • GPS Coordinates: Precise latitude and longitude from your device's GPS receiver
  • Reverse Geocoding Results: State and city names derived from coordinates using Apple's CLGeocoder service
  • Location Accuracy: The GPS accuracy measurement associated with each location update
  • Timestamps: The date and time of each location detection

How Location Data is Used: GPS coordinates are processed on your device to determine which state you are in. This information is used to count your presence days per state for tax compliance purposes. All location processing occurs locally on your iPhone.

1.2 Data Stored on Your Device

The following data is stored locally on your device:

  • State Presence Records: The states you have visited, dates of presence, and associated metadata (day type, confidence score, optional notes)
  • App Settings: Your preferences, home state selection, notification settings, and display options
  • Subscription Status: Information about your subscription tier (managed by Apple)
  • Cryptographic Keys: Keys used for report verification, stored securely in iOS Keychain

1.3 Information We Do Not Collect

We do not collect, store, or have access to:

  • Your name, email address, or contact information
  • Your location data on any external server
  • Your movement patterns or travel history
  • Any data that could identify you personally
  • Analytics or usage data

2. How Your Data is Stored

2.1 On-Device Storage

All state presence data is stored locally on your iPhone using:

  • UserDefaults: For app settings and state presence records. This data is stored in your app's sandboxed container and is not accessible to other apps.
  • iOS Keychain: For cryptographic signing keys used in accountant report verification. Keychain provides hardware-level security and encryption.

Your data is protected by iOS security features including device encryption, app sandboxing, and biometric authentication (if enabled on your device).

2.2 No Cloud Storage

Days in State does not use iCloud sync, cloud databases, or any external servers to store your data. Your state presence records exist solely on the device where they were created.

Important: Because data is stored only on your device, uninstalling the app or switching devices will result in data loss unless you export your records first.

3. Third-Party Services

3.1 Apple Services

Days in State uses the following Apple services:

  • Core Location: For GPS location detection (processed on-device)
  • CLGeocoder: Apple's reverse geocoding service converts coordinates to state names. Coordinates are sent to Apple for this purpose and are subject to Apple's Privacy Policy
  • StoreKit: For subscription management (handled entirely by Apple)
  • Push Notifications: For daily check-in reminders (no personal data transmitted)

3.2 No Third-Party Analytics or Advertising

Days in State does not include any third-party analytics SDKs, advertising networks, or tracking tools. We do not share any data with third parties for marketing or advertising purposes.

4. Data Sharing

4.1 Accountant Sharing Feature

The Pro subscription includes a feature to share state presence reports with your tax professional. When you use this feature:

  • You generate a report file on your device
  • Reports include cryptographic signatures for verification
  • You control how the report is shared (email, AirDrop, etc.)
  • We do not transmit or have access to shared reports

4.2 Export Features

You can export your state presence data for personal use or tax preparation. Exported files are created locally on your device, and you control their distribution.

5. Your Rights and Choices

5.1 Location Permission

You can control location access at any time through iOS Settings. Options include:

  • Always: Allows background state detection (recommended)
  • While Using: Only detects states when app is open
  • Never: Disables location detection (app functionality limited)

5.2 Data Deletion

You have complete control over your data and can delete it at any time:

  • Delete Individual Records: Remove specific state presence entries from Settings
  • Clear All Data: Use the "Reset All Data" option in Settings to delete all state presence records
  • Uninstall the App: Removing the app deletes all associated data from your device

Because we do not store your data on any servers, there is no external data to request or delete. All your data exists solely on your device under your control.

5.3 Data Export

You can export all your state presence data at any time using the Export feature. Available formats include CSV, PDF, and formats compatible with popular tax software.

6. Children's Privacy

Days in State is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. The App is intended for adults managing their tax obligations.

7. Security

We take the security of your data seriously:

  • All data is stored in iOS's sandboxed app container
  • Cryptographic keys are stored in the iOS Keychain with hardware-level protection
  • No data is transmitted to external servers
  • Report signatures use cryptographic verification

Your data inherits the security protections of your iOS device, including device encryption and biometric authentication.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any material changes by updating the "Last updated" date at the top of this policy and, where appropriate, through in-app notification.

We encourage you to review this Privacy Policy periodically to stay informed about how we are protecting your information.

9. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or our privacy practices, please contact us at:

Email: bestdaylabs@gmail.com

10. California Privacy Rights

California residents may have additional rights under the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA). However, because Days in State does not collect personal information on external servers and all data remains on your device, there is no personal information for us to disclose, sell, or delete. You maintain complete control over all your data.

11. International Users

Days in State is designed for tracking presence in US states for US tax compliance. Because all data is stored on your device and we do not operate servers, there is no cross-border data transfer to consider. The app functions the same regardless of your location.
