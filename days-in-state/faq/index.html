Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about Days in State.

Getting Started

What is Days in State?
Days in State is an automatic state presence tracking app for iOS. It uses GPS to record which states you visit each day, for multi-state tax documentation.
How does automatic tracking work?
Days in State uses iOS significant location change monitoring. It runs in the background, detects state border crossings automatically. No manual logging needed.
Does this drain my battery?
No. Days in State uses iOS's most battery-efficient location monitoring. It only wakes up on significant location changes.
What if I forget to open the app?
Background tracking works even when the app is closed. You can also manually add entries for any days you might have missed.

Privacy & Data

Is my location data private?
Yes. All data stays on your device and optionally your private iCloud. No servers, no accounts, no tracking on our end. Read our Privacy Policy for full details.
Does my data leave my phone?
Only if you enable iCloud backup (which goes to YOUR private iCloud) or export a CSV. We never see your data.
Can I delete my data?
Yes. Go to Settings > Delete All Data to erase everything, or simply delete the app from your device.

Features

Can I edit or delete individual records?
Yes. Tap any day in the calendar to see details and make changes. All edits are logged in the audit trail.
How do I export data for my CPA?
This is a Pro feature. Go to Settings > Export Data to generate a CSV with dates, locations, day counts, and confidence scores.
What does confidence score mean?
The confidence score indicates the evidentiary strength of each record:
  • 1.0 (100%) - GPS detected + user confirmed
  • 0.9 (90%) - GPS detected automatically
  • 0.7 (70%) - Manual entry
Higher scores carry more evidentiary weight.
Does the app work internationally?
Yes. Days in State tracks US states and international countries using ISO 3166 standards.

Pricing & Subscriptions

What's included in the free plan?
The free plan includes automatic state tracking and the last 30 days of history.
What does Pro add?
Pro adds full tax year history, calendar view, CPA-ready exports, accountant sharing, and iCloud backup.
How much does Pro cost?
$4.99/month, $19.99/year, or $39.99 one-time lifetime purchase.
How do I restore my purchase on a new device?
Go to Settings > Restore Purchases in the app. You must use the same Apple ID you used for the original purchase.
How do I cancel my subscription?
Subscriptions are managed through Apple:
  • Open the Settings app on your iPhone
  • Tap your name at the top
  • Tap Subscriptions
  • Find Days in State and tap Cancel Subscription
You'll continue to have Pro access until the end of your billing period.

Tax Information

Does the app provide tax advice?
No. Days in State tracks where you were and when. Always consult a qualified tax professional for tax-related decisions.
What is the 183-day rule?
Many states consider you a tax resident after 183 days of presence. The specific rules vary by state. Read our detailed guide on the 183-day rule.
Can this app be used as proof in a tax audit?
The app provides documented records with GPS verification and confidence scores. Consult your tax professional about evidentiary requirements in your jurisdiction.
