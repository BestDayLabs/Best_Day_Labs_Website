1. Description of Service

Days in State is a mobile application that tracks your presence in US states using GPS location data. The App counts the number of days you spend in each state to help you monitor potential multi-state tax obligations.

The App provides tools for tracking, reporting, and exporting state presence data. All data is stored locally on your device.

3. Accuracy of Data

While we strive for accuracy, the App's state detection depends on:

  • GPS signal quality and availability
  • Apple's reverse geocoding service
  • Your device's location settings
  • Environmental factors affecting GPS accuracy

You are responsible for reviewing and verifying all state presence records for accuracy. The App provides tools to manually add, edit, or delete records to correct any errors.

We do not guarantee that the App will detect every state transition or that recorded locations will be 100% accurate. Near state borders, GPS inaccuracies may result in incorrect state attribution.
