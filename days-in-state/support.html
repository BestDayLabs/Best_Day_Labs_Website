Frequently Asked Questions

Getting Started

Setup is simple:

  1. Download Days in State from the App Store
  2. Open the app and complete the brief onboarding
  3. When prompted, allow location access (select "Always" for best results)
  4. That's it! The app will automatically start tracking your state presence

No locations to configure, no accounts to create. The app works immediately.

"Always" permission allows the app to detect state changes even when you're not actively using it. This is essential for accurate tracking because:

  • You might cross state lines while the app is closed
  • Overnight stays need to be recorded
  • Background detection ensures you don't miss any days

If you select "While Using," the app can only track states when you have it open, which may result in missed days and incomplete records.

Privacy note: Your location data never leaves your device. We don't have servers that receive or store your location.

Days in State is designed for minimal battery impact. We use iOS's "Significant Location Changes" feature, which only wakes the app when you've moved approximately 500 meters or more. This is much more battery-efficient than continuous GPS tracking.

Most users report no noticeable impact on battery life. If you're concerned, you can check battery usage in iOS Settings → Battery.

Tracking & Data

State detection is generally very accurate, but depends on several factors:

  • GPS signal quality: Better accuracy outdoors with clear sky view
  • Distance from state borders: Detections very close to borders may occasionally be attributed to the wrong state
  • Urban canyons: Tall buildings can affect GPS accuracy

Each record includes a confidence score based on GPS accuracy. We recommend reviewing records near state borders and using the daily check-in feature for the most reliable documentation.

You can always manually add, edit, or delete records to correct any errors.

You can manually correct any record:

  1. Go to the Calendar view (Pro feature)
  2. Tap on the day you want to correct
  3. Use "Add Manual Entry" to add a missing state
  4. Or tap an existing record to edit or delete it

Manual entries are marked differently in reports and receive the highest confidence score, as they represent user-verified data.

The app records each state you visit on a given day as a separate presence record. For example, if you drive from California to Nevada, both states will show a record for that day.

Each record is classified by type:

  • Full Day: Standard presence
  • Overnight Stay: You slept in this state
  • Partial Day: Brief visit, typically arrival or departure
  • Travel Through: Transit only (e.g., airport layover)

The app automatically classifies days based on patterns, but you can manually override the classification.

Tax & Thresholds

Many states use a 183-day rule as one factor in determining tax residency. If you spend 183 days or more in a state during a tax year, you may be considered a resident for tax purposes and could owe state income tax.

Important: The 183-day rule is just one of many factors states consider. Other factors include:

  • Where your permanent home (domicile) is located
  • Where your family lives
  • Where you're registered to vote
  • Where your driver's license is issued
  • Where your bank accounts are located

Each state has different rules. Some states (like Texas, Florida, and Washington) have no state income tax at all.

Always consult with a tax professional about your specific situation. Days in State is a tracking tool, not tax advice.

Subscription & Billing

When you start a Pro subscription, you get 90 days of full access at no charge:

  • No payment is taken when you start the trial
  • You have full access to all Pro features during the trial
  • You can cancel anytime before the trial ends to avoid charges
  • If you don't cancel, your subscription begins at the end of the trial

The 90-day trial is available once per Apple ID.

Subscriptions are managed through Apple:

  1. Open the Settings app on your iPhone
  2. Tap your name at the top
  3. Tap "Subscriptions"
  4. Find "Days in State" and tap it
  5. Tap "Cancel Subscription"

You'll keep Pro access until the end of your current billing period. After that, you'll revert to the free tier but your data will remain on your device.

Your data is safe! Canceling your subscription does not delete your data. Here's what happens:

  • All your state presence records remain on your device
  • The app continues to track states automatically
  • You'll only be able to view the last 30 days of history
  • Pro features (exports, alerts, calendar) become unavailable

If you resubscribe later, you'll regain access to your full history.

Troubleshooting

If the app isn't tracking properly, try these steps:

  1. Check location permission: Go to iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Location Services → Days in State. Ensure it's set to "Always."
  2. Enable Background App Refresh: iOS Settings → General → Background App Refresh → Days in State (On)
  3. Disable Low Power Mode: Low Power Mode can restrict background location access
  4. Restart the app: Close and reopen Days in State
  5. Restart your iPhone: A fresh restart can resolve many issues

If problems persist, try uninstalling and reinstalling the app. Note: this will delete your existing data, so export first if needed.

Because all data is stored locally on your device for privacy, it does not automatically transfer to a new phone.

Before switching phones:

  • Use the Export feature to save your data (CSV or PDF)
  • Store the export file somewhere safe (email, cloud storage, etc.)

On your new phone:

  • If you restored from an iCloud backup, your app data should transfer automatically
  • If data didn't transfer, you may need to manually re-enter important records or import from your export

We're exploring secure backup options for future versions while maintaining our privacy-first approach.
