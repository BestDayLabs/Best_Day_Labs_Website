The stats that move your score

Not a scorecard. Sandy focuses on the handful of numbers that predict scoring — and shows each one with its denominator, so a partial round is never misleading.

Greens in regulation

How often you give yourself a real birdie look.

Putts per GIR

Your putting, measured the honest way.

Scrambling

Up-and-down rate when you miss the green.

Green-light drives

Drives that leave you a clean look — the tee stat that matters.

Driving distance

Measured from your GPS taps, shown honestly banded.

Score

To par, per hole and per round, never blending 9s and 18s.

Three steps, every round

1
Arrive. Tap Play. Sandy knows the course if anyone's played it — or you add it for everyone. Pick tees and holes, Start.
2
Play. Tap Tee, Tap Drive, Log Hole — by “Hey Siri,” by talking to Sandy in the app, or by tapping it in. Just say it: “Hit the green, two putts, made par.”
3
Finish. End the round and your stats and trends build themselves.
