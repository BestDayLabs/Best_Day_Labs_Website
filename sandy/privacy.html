Sandy — Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 28, 2026 · Best Day Labs, LLC

Sandy is a golf stats tracker. We keep it simple and we don't sell your data, show ads, or use third-party tracking or analytics. This policy explains what we collect, why, and your choices.

What we collect

  • Account. By default you use Sandy with an anonymous device identifier — no name or email required. If you choose Sign in with Apple, we receive the name and email you authorize so your rounds sync across your devices.
  • Your golf data. The rounds you log — scores, putts, greens in regulation, scrambling, driving distance, course and tee details, and any voice notes you add.
  • Location. When you tap “Tee Off” / “Drive,” or to recognize the course you're at, Sandy reads your GPS location. Location is used only while you're using the app for these features — Sandy does not track your location in the background.
  • Voice. When you use voice entry, the short audio clip is sent to our processors to transcribe it and interpret the result; the resulting text is saved with that hole. Audio clips are used to produce the transcript and are not retained after processing.

How we use it

To provide the app: record your rounds, compute your stats and trends, recognize courses, surface your past notes for a hole, and sync your data to your account. That's it.

Community course library

Sandy builds its course library from real play instead of a paid course database. When you finish a round, the course geometry only — the course's location, the par of each hole, and approximate tee-box coordinates — is contributed to a shared, anonymous community course so the next golfer who shows up there already has it. This shared course data carries no personal identity: it is not linked to your name or account publicly. Your scores, putts, notes, and personal stats are never shared — only the course's physical layout. This is how a first-time visitor benefits from a course others have already played.

Who processes it (sub-processors)

  • Supabase — hosting, database, and authentication for syncing your account.
  • OpenAI — speech-to-text transcription of your voice clips.
  • Anthropic — interpreting the transcript into the right stats.
  • Apple — Sign in with Apple, and processing optional tips (in-app purchases). We never receive your payment-card details.

We do not sell your data, share it for advertising, or use third-party advertising or analytics SDKs.

Storage, retention & deletion

Your data is stored on your device and in our Supabase backend, tied to your account ID. You can remove all data on your device from Account → Developer → Clear all data (in development builds) and you can request deletion of your synced account data at any time by emailing us at the address below — we'll delete it.

Children

Sandy is not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect data from them.

Your choices

You control Location and Microphone access in iOS Settings; both are optional (you can always tap your data in by hand). You can stay anonymous, sign in, or sign out, and request deletion anytime.

Contact

Questions or deletion requests: privacy@bestdaylabs.com.
