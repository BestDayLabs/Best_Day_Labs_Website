Sandy is a dead-simple golf stats tracker. Talk to it, tap twice for your drive, done. Need a hand? Email support@bestdaylabs.com — we read every message.

Getting started

1 · Arrive — Tap Play. If anyone's played the course before, Sandy recognizes it; otherwise you add it. Pick your tees and holes, then Start Round.

2 · Play — Three actions per hole: Tap Tee, Tap Drive (for distance), and Log Hole. Enter them three ways — “Hey Siri,” “Talk to Sandy” in the app, or tap it in.

3 · Finish — Tap End round. Your stats and trends build themselves.

Frequently asked

How do I log a hole by voice?

Tap the mic and just say it: “Hit the green, two putts, made par.” Sandy's AI turns your words into the right stats and asks if anything's missing. You can also say “Hey Siri, log hole.”

Do I need an Apple Watch?

No. Sandy is fully functional on iPhone alone. The Watch app is an optional companion.

Why is my driving distance approximate?

Distance is measured from your “Tee Off” tap to your “Drive” tap using your phone's GPS, which is accurate to a few yards at best. Sandy shows it banded (e.g. “~280 yds”) rather than falsely precise, and ignores low-accuracy readings.

Is it really free?

Yes — no ads, no subscription, no paywall. If you'd like to support development, there's an optional tip jar.

How do I delete my data?

You can clear data on your device from the app, and you can email us to delete your synced account data at any time. See our Privacy Policy.
