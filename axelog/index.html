Features

Everything You Need to Level Up

Tools designed to help you practice smarter and track your progress over time.

Practice Streaks

Visual 7-day streak indicator keeps you motivated and builds consistent daily practice habits.

Session Logging

Timer-based sessions with multiple types: Freeform, Technique, Song Work, Jamming, and Planned.

Long-term Goals

Set and track up to 3 active practice goals to stay focused on your musical journey.

Song Library

Manage songs with status tracking, BPM, tuning, and difficulty ratings. From learning to mastered.

Audio & Video Recording

Record practice sessions with a tape-deck style interface. Document your progress over time.

12-Week Heatmap

Visual practice activity history shows your dedication at a glance. See patterns, identify gaps.

Analytics Dashboard

Track total practice time, session averages, and weekly stats. Know exactly where you stand.

Daily Reminders

Customizable notification reminders help you never miss a practice session.

Custom App Icons

Choose from 19 custom app icons to personalize your home screen. Match your style.
