Privacy Policy

Effective Date: April 1, 2025

This Privacy Policy explains how Best Day Labs ("we", "us", or "our") handles your information when you use the Nerve mobile application (the "App").

1. Data Collection

We do not collect, transmit, or store any personal data. All data generated by the App is stored locally on your device. We do not use analytics, we do not track your usage, we do not require user accounts, and we do not operate any external servers to process your data.

2. Apple Frameworks & Screen Time APIs

The App utilizes Apple's Screen Time APIs (specifically FamilyControls, ManagedSettings, and DeviceActivity) to provide its core functionality of blocking and unblocking apps on your device. All data accessed through these frameworks remains strictly on your device and never leaves it. Best Day Labs has no access to your screen time data, browsing history, or app usage statistics.

3. Purchases and Payments

All subscriptions and in-app purchases are handled entirely and securely by Apple. We do not collect, process, or store your payment information. Your purchase history is tied to your Apple ID and managed by Apple's App Store.

4. Third-Party Services

The App does not integrate with or use any third-party SDKs, tracking services, or advertising networks.

5. Children's Privacy

We do not knowingly collect or solicit any information from anyone, including children. Because all data is stored locally and we operate no external servers, no child's personal data is ever transmitted to us.

6. Contact Us

If you have any questions or concerns about this Privacy Policy or your privacy while using Nerve, please contact us at:
support@bestdaylabs.com
