Frequently Asked Questions

How do I cancel my subscription?

All subscriptions are securely managed by Apple. To cancel, open the Settings app on your iPhone, tap your name at the top, tap Subscriptions, select Nerve, and tap Cancel Subscription.

Nerve isn't blocking my apps. What do I do?

Ensure that Nerve has been granted the necessary Screen Time permissions. You may need to open your iPhone Settings, go to Screen Time, and ensure Nerve is fully allowed to manage your device activity.

I found a bug. How do I report it?

Please email us at the address above with a description of the bug, what you were doing when it happened, and your device model/iOS version.
