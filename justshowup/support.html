Frequently Asked Questions

How does automatic detection work?
JustShowUp uses iOS geofencing. When you add a place, the app sets up a geofence around that location. iOS notifies the app when you arrive or leave, without the app needing to run continuously. This is battery-efficient and happens automatically.
Does JustShowUp drain my battery?
No. JustShowUp relies on iOS geofencing, which is one of the most battery-efficient location features available. The app does not continuously track your GPS. It only wakes up when iOS detects you've entered or left a configured place.
Do I need to open the app to record a visit?
No. Detection happens in the background. You don't need to open the app, tap anything, or remember to check in. Just go to your place and JustShowUp handles the rest.
What counts as a place?
Anything you return to regularly that matters to you. A gym, a yoga studio, a therapist's office, a church, a school, a park. If you go there and it means something, it's a place.
Is my location data private?
Yes, completely. All place data stays on your device. There are no accounts, no cloud sync, and no servers storing your places. The App uses PostHog for anonymized product analytics and the Meta SDK for ad attribution, but neither receives your place names, addresses, or visit history. See our Privacy Policy for details. What's the difference between Free and Pro?
Free lets you track up to 2 active places with the full mosaic calendar and daily timeline. Pro unlocks unlimited active places, deeper reflections, and a fuller calendar history. Pro is available as an annual subscription or a one-time lifetime purchase.
The app isn't detecting my visits. What should I do?
Check the following:
  • Make sure Location Services are enabled: Settings → Privacy & Security → Location Services → JustShowUp → "Always"
  • Ensure the place radius is large enough to cover the location
  • Verify that Background App Refresh is enabled: Settings → General → Background App Refresh → JustShowUp
  • Restart the app or your device if the issue persists
If none of these resolve it, please contact us.
What devices and iOS versions are supported?
JustShowUp requires iOS 17.0 or later and runs on iPhone only. iPad and Mac are not currently supported.
How do I restore my purchase on a new device?
Open JustShowUp, go to Settings, and tap "Restore Purchases." Make sure you're signed into the same Apple ID you used for the original purchase. Your Pro status will be restored automatically.
How do I cancel my subscription?
Subscriptions are managed through Apple:
  • Open the Settings app on your iPhone
  • Tap your name at the top
  • Tap Subscriptions
  • Find JustShowUp and tap Cancel Subscription
You'll keep Pro access until the end of your current billing period.
Can I delete my data?
Yes. You can delete individual places and their visit history from within the app. To remove all data, go to Settings and use the delete option, or simply delete the app from your device.
