Questions

What counts as a place?
Anything you return to. A gym, a studio, a therapist's office, a church, a school, a park bench. If you go there regularly and it matters to you, it's a place.
Does the app track me all the time?
No. JustShowUp uses iOS geofencing to notice when you arrive at or leave a specific place you've set up. It does not continuously track your location or record where you go outside of your places.
Does my data leave my phone?
Your place data (names, addresses, coordinates, visit history) never leaves your device. There are no accounts and no cloud sync. The App does use anonymized product analytics (PostHog) and ad attribution (Meta SDK), but neither receives any place data. See our Privacy Policy for full details.
Do I need to check in manually?
No. JustShowUp detects your arrival and departure automatically. You don't need to open the app, tap a button, or remember anything. Just show up.
What happens on the free plan?
You can track up to 2 active places with all core features: automatic detection, the mosaic calendar, and daily timelines. Upgrade to Pro for unlimited places, deeper reflections, and a fuller history.
