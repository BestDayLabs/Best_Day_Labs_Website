# JustShowUp Website Brief

## Objective
Build a simple, premium, App Store-compliant marketing site for JustShowUp that:

- explains what the app is in one glance
- helps a visitor understand what a `place` is
- supports App Store review expectations with clear support and privacy pages
- feels visually aligned with the iOS app
- does not overcomplicate the product with feature sprawl

This is not a growth-hacker landing page. It should feel calm, trustworthy, and intentional.

## Brand

- Product: `JustShowUp`
- Tagline: `You were there. We noticed.`
- Voice: calm, factual, warm, non-judgmental
- Avoid:
  - productivity-app energy
  - compliance/proof framing
  - guilt, streaks, pressure, or behavior shaming

## Audience

People who want a quiet record of how they spend time in places that matter:

- gym
- studio
- therapy
- worship
- school
- park
- any personally meaningful recurring place

## Core Message

JustShowUp automatically notices when you return to the places that matter and reflects that rhythm back to you in a calm, beautiful calendar.

## Site Structure

### 1. Home
The home page should do almost all of the work.

Sections:

1. Hero
2. What a place is
3. How it works
4. Reflection/calendar showcase
5. Privacy promise
6. Pricing
7. FAQ
8. Footer

### 2. Privacy
Simple, plain-language privacy page.

Must clearly say:

- location data stays on device
- no accounts
- no analytics SDKs
- no cloud sync
- no selling data
- only StoreKit-related Apple commerce flows leave device

### 3. Support
Simple support page with:

- support email
- FAQ
- device/OS compatibility
- notification and location troubleshooting

### 4. Optional

- Terms page
- Press kit / media kit

## Home Page Requirements

### Hero
Goal: explain the product immediately.

Must include:

- app name
- tagline
- 1 short sentence explaining the product
- App Store download button
- 1 strong iPhone mockup or motion preview

Recommended hero copy direction:

- `A calm record of the places that shape your life.`
- `JustShowUp notices when you return, then reflects it back in your calendar.`

### What a Place Is
This section is mandatory.

Users do not automatically understand what a `place` means in the product.

Explain plainly:

- a place can be a gym
- a studio
- a therapy office
- a worship space
- a school
- a park
- or any room you return to regularly

This should be visual. Use 5-6 soft category cards or medallions, not dense copy.

### How It Works
Keep it to 3 steps:

1. Add a place
2. Let JustShowUp notice when you arrive and leave
3. See your month take shape in the calendar and reflections

Do not add more steps.

### Reflection / Calendar Showcase
Show the emotional center of the app.

Must feature:

- the custom mosaic calendar
- a daily timeline detail
- a reflection card or overlay

This section should answer:

- why would I open this app?
- why is this different from a tracker?

### Privacy Promise
Make this visually distinct and extremely clear.

Suggested framing:

- `Your data stays with you.`
- `No accounts. No analytics. No cloud sync.`

### Pricing
Keep pricing simple and consistent with the app:

- Free: up to 2 active places
- Pro Annual
- Pro Lifetime

The value story should focus on:

- more active places
- a fuller calendar
- deeper reflections over time

Do not lead with exports or proof.

### FAQ
Keep to the obvious questions:

- What counts as a place?
- Does the app track me all the time?
- Does my data leave my phone?
- Do I need to check in manually?
- What happens on the free plan?

## Visual Direction

The site should feel like the app:

- dark navy and warm off-white surfaces
- amber, sage, coral, and soft blue accents
- strong spacing
- premium iOS-inspired composition
- subtle gradients and bloom, not flashy animation

Do not make the website look like:

- a SaaS dashboard
- a startup template
- a generic productivity app

## App Store Compliance Requirements

The website must include:

- working Privacy Policy page
- working Support page
- truthful screenshots and copy
- no misleading claims
- no fake Apple affiliation
- no references to unsupported platforms or features

Do not claim:

- medical benefits
- therapy outcomes
- legal proof
- employment compliance
- health diagnostics

## Design Constraints

- Mobile-first
- Fast load
- Accessible contrast
- Dynamic, but not animation-heavy
- No auto-playing loud video
- No newsletter popup
- No intrusive cookie banner unless legally required by deployment region

## Technical Recommendation

Use a simple static or mostly-static stack:

- Next.js, Astro, or plain static HTML/CSS
- lightweight CMS optional, not required
- optimized image handling
- no heavy client-side app shell

## Deliverables

Ask the designer/developer for:

1. Desktop and mobile homepage designs
2. Privacy page
3. Support page
4. App Store badge treatment
5. Screenshot/mockup system
6. Final build-ready assets and CSS tokens

## Copy Guidance

The best site copy is:

- short
- plain
- observant
- visually driven

The site should sound like:

- `JustShowUp notices when you return.`
- `Your month starts to take shape.`
- `The places that matter gather here.`

Not like:

- `Optimize your attendance`
- `Track productivity`
- `Build streaks`
- `Prove compliance`

## Immediate Creative Direction

If there is only enough budget/time for one page, make:

- one excellent homepage
- one privacy page
- one support page

That is enough for launch.
