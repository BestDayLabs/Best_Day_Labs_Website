Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 8, 2026

Your location data stays on your device. We never see it, store it, or have access to it. There are no accounts, no analytics SDKs, and no cloud sync.

Overview

JustShowUp ("the App") is designed with privacy as a foundational principle. We believe your location data is deeply personal. The App is architected so that we never have access to it.

Data We Collect

We do not collect any personal data.

The App processes location data entirely on your device. This data is:

  • Stored locally on your iPhone
  • Never transmitted to our servers
  • Never shared with third parties
  • Never synced to the cloud

Location Data

The App uses iOS geofencing to detect when you arrive at or leave places you have configured. This location data:

  • Is processed entirely on your device
  • Is stored only on your device
  • Is never sent to us or any third party
  • Can be deleted at any time from within the App

JustShowUp does not continuously track your location. It only monitors arrivals and departures at the specific places you configure.

No Accounts

JustShowUp does not require or offer account creation. There is no sign-up, no login, no email collection, and no user profile.

No Analytics

We do not use any analytics SDKs or services within the App. We do not track:

  • How you use the App
  • What features you access
  • How often you open the App
  • Any usage patterns

Note: This website uses Google Analytics for page visit statistics. The App itself contains no analytics.

No Cloud Sync

Your place and visit data is stored exclusively on your device. There is no cloud sync, no backup service, and no remote storage of any kind.

Third-Party Services

The App uses the following Apple services:

  • Core Location — For geofence-based arrival and departure detection (on-device)
  • StoreKit — For in-app purchases (Apple handles payment)

We do not use any third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking services in the App.

In-App Purchases

Purchases are processed by Apple through StoreKit. We do not receive or store any payment information. This is the only data flow that leaves your device, and it goes directly to Apple—not to us.

Data Retention

Since we don't collect your data, there is nothing to retain on our end. Your data remains on your device until you choose to delete it.

You can delete all your data at any time:

  • Within the App via Settings
  • By deleting the App from your device

Children's Privacy

The App is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect any information from children, since we do not collect information from anyone.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page and updating the "Last updated" date.
