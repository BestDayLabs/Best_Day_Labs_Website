Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 19, 2026

Your place data—names, addresses, coordinates, visit history—stays on your device. We never see it, store it, or have access to it. There are no accounts and no cloud sync. The App does use limited analytics and ad attribution services, described below.

Overview

JustShowUp ("the App") is designed with privacy as a foundational principle. We believe your location data is deeply personal. The App is architected so that we never have access to it.

Data We Collect

We do not collect any place data. Your place names, addresses, coordinates, and visit history are processed and stored entirely on your device. This data is:

  • Stored locally on your iPhone
  • Never transmitted to our servers
  • Never synced to the cloud

The App does collect limited, anonymized analytics events and may share your device advertising identifier with Meta for ad measurement. See the sections below for details.

Location Data

The App uses iOS geofencing to detect when you arrive at or leave places you have configured. This location data:

  • Is processed entirely on your device
  • Is stored only on your device
  • Is never sent to us or any third party
  • Can be deleted at any time from within the App

JustShowUp does not continuously track your location. It only monitors arrivals and departures at the specific places you configure.

No Accounts

JustShowUp does not require or offer account creation. There is no sign-up, no login, no email collection, and no user profile.

Analytics

The App uses PostHog for product analytics. PostHog collects anonymized funnel events—for example, when a paywall is shown or a purchase is started. This helps us understand how people use the App so we can improve it.

PostHog does not receive:

  • Place names, addresses, or coordinates
  • Visit history or calendar data
  • Any information about where you go

Analytics data is sent to PostHog's servers (us.i.posthog.com). No place data is included.

Note: This website uses Google Analytics for page visit statistics.

Tracking & Advertising

The App uses the Facebook (Meta) SDK for ad attribution only. This measures which ads led to app installs and subscriptions.

  • The App requests tracking permission via Apple's App Tracking Transparency (ATT) framework
  • If you grant permission, your device's advertising identifier (IDFA) is shared with Meta for ad measurement
  • You can deny or revoke this permission at any time in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking
  • No place data is shared with Meta
  • No user profiles are built by us
  • No data is sold to data brokers

No Cloud Sync

Your place and visit data is stored exclusively on your device. There is no cloud sync, no backup service, and no remote storage of any kind. Exports (PDF/CSV) are generated and shared locally.

Third-Party Services

The App uses the following services:

  • Core Location (Apple) — For geofence-based arrival and departure detection (on-device)
  • StoreKit (Apple) — For in-app purchases (Apple handles payment)
  • PostHog — Anonymized product analytics (no place data sent)
  • Facebook/Meta SDK — Ad attribution only (IDFA with your permission, no place data sent)

In-App Purchases

Purchases are processed by Apple through StoreKit. We do not receive or store any payment information.

Data Retention

Your place data remains on your device until you choose to delete it. We do not store it on our servers. PostHog retains anonymized analytics events per their retention policy. Meta retains ad attribution data per their data policy.

You can delete all your data at any time:

  • Within the App via Settings
  • By deleting the App from your device

Children's Privacy

The App is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page and updating the "Last updated" date.
