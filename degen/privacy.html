Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 10, 2026

The short version: Degen has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. Your portfolios, trades and progress are stored on your iPhone, and we don't collect them. To show live prices, the app asks public market-data services for prices — those requests don't include anything about you.

Who we are

Degen (“the App”) is made by Best Day Labs, LLC (“Best Day Labs,” “we,” “us”). This policy explains what information the App handles and how. If you have questions, contact us at support@bestdaylabs.com.

Information we collect

We do not collect personal information through the App. There is no sign-up, login, email, phone number or user profile, and we do not operate servers that receive your app data.

Information stored on your device

To make the game work, the App stores the following locally on your iPhone:

  • The username (“handle”) you choose, and your onboarding answers (for example, what you like to trade)
  • Your virtual portfolios: virtual cash balance, positions, orders, trade history and portfolio history
  • Game progress: XP, level, streak, badges and daily challenges
  • App settings, such as whether the Face ID lock is on

This data stays on your device. It is not sent to us. Deleting the App deletes it. Like other app data, it may be included in your own device backups (for example, an iCloud or computer backup you control), which are governed by Apple's policies, not ours.

Market data requests

To show live prices, charts and markets, the App connects directly from your device to public market-data services, including CoinGecko, Coinbase, GeckoTerminal, DEX Screener, Jupiter, Binance (public market data), Hyperliquid, PumpPortal and Polymarket, and loads token and market images from the addresses those services provide.

These requests ask for public market information — for example, “the price of this coin.” They do not include your handle, portfolio, trades or any identifier we create. As with any internet request, these services can see your device's IP address and basic technical details (such as the type of connection), and they handle that under their own privacy policies. We do not control those services and do not receive this information.

Permissions and device features

  • Notifications (optional). If you allow notifications, the App schedules local reminders on your device — for example, a streak reminder or a heads-up when a prediction market you hold is starting or closing. These are scheduled by iOS on your phone; no notification data is sent to us, and we don't use push notification servers.
  • Face ID / Touch ID (optional). If you turn on the app lock, iOS checks your face or fingerprint and tells the App only whether it succeeded. The App never receives or stores any biometric data.
  • Photos (optional, add-only). If you save a trade or portfolio card, the App asks to add that image or video to your photo library. It cannot read your existing photos.
  • Sharing. When you share a card, it goes through the standard iOS share sheet to the destination you choose.

Purchases

Degen Pro is an optional auto-renewing subscription sold through Apple's App Store. Apple processes the payment; we never receive your payment details. The App uses Apple's StoreKit to check whether your subscription is active. Apple may share limited, non-identifying purchase information with us through App Store Connect (such as sales and subscription counts), as it does for all developers.

No tracking, ads or analytics in the App

The App contains no third-party analytics, advertising or tracking SDKs. We do not track you across other companies' apps or websites, we do not build advertising profiles, and we do not sell or share personal information.

Apple may provide us aggregated app statistics (such as downloads and crash reports) if you've opted in to share analytics with app developers in your iPhone settings. These are anonymized by Apple.

This website

This website (bestdaylabs.com) uses Google Analytics to understand basic visit statistics, such as page views. Google may set cookies and process your IP address for this purpose under its own policies. You can opt out using browser settings or Google's opt-out tools. The website is separate from the App and receives no app data.

Children

Degen is not directed to children and is intended for users 17 and older. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Because the App collects no personal information, there is none for us to hold; if you believe a child has contacted us directly with personal information, email us and we'll delete it.

Data retention and deletion

Your app data stays on your device until you delete it. You can reset a portfolio inside the App, and deleting the App removes everything it stored. If you email us, we keep your message only as long as needed to help you and then delete it.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example, under the GDPR in Europe or the CCPA in California), you may have rights to access, correct, delete or object to the processing of your personal information. Because we don't collect personal information through the App, we generally hold nothing to provide or delete — but if you contact us, we'll respond to any request about information you've sent us directly.

Security

Your data is protected by your iPhone's built-in security, and you can add Face ID protection inside the App. Connections to market-data services use encrypted HTTPS/WSS where the service supports it.

Changes to this policy

If we change how the App handles information, we'll update this page and the “Last updated” date. Material changes will be noted in the App's release notes.
