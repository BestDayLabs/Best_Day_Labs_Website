Good to know

The short version: it's a game. A really realistic one.

Is any of the money real?
No. Every dollar in Degen is virtual. You can't deposit, withdraw or cash out, and nothing you do in the app buys or sells real crypto.
Where do the prices come from?
Public market data from sources like CoinGecko, Coinbase, GeckoTerminal, DEX Screener, Jupiter, Hyperliquid and Polymarket. Prices are live but can occasionally be delayed or briefly off.
Is this financial advice?
No. Degen is entertainment and practice. Paper-trading results don't predict real results — real trading has risks the game can't fully capture.
Do I need an account?
No. There's no sign-up and no login. Your portfolios and progress live on your iPhone.
How do I cancel Pro?
Anytime, in Settings → your name → Subscriptions. Cancel at least 24 hours before the trial or period ends and you won't be charged again.
