How can we help?

Quick answers below. Still stuck? Email us — a real person reads every message.

Subscriptions

How do I cancel Degen Pro?
Open your iPhone's Settings → tap your name → Subscriptions → Degen. Cancel at least 24 hours before your trial or billing period ends to avoid the next charge. You keep Pro until the period you've paid for ends.
I paid but don't have Pro
On the paywall, tap Restore. Make sure you're signed in with the same Apple ID you subscribed with.
How do I use a promo code?
Tap Redeem code at the bottom of the Pro screen and enter it. Pro unlocks as soon as Apple confirms the code.
Can I get a refund?
Purchases go through Apple, so refunds are handled by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Playing

Is any of this real money?
No. Everything is virtual — you can't deposit, withdraw or cash out, and the app never buys or sells real crypto.
I lost everything. Can I start over?
Yes. Go to Profile → Reset. Free accounts get one reset; Pro resets are unlimited and start you with $150K.
A price or chart looks wrong
Prices come from public market-data services and can occasionally lag or glitch, especially on brand-new or very thin memecoins. Close and reopen the coin to reload it. If something stays wrong, email us the coin and a screenshot.
Why did my stop loss fill below my trigger?
Just like real apps, a trigger sends a market order — if the price gaps past your level, it fills where the price actually is.
How do I turn the Face ID lock on or off?
Profile → Face ID lock. If you're locked out, use your device passcode when prompted.
Will my progress transfer to a new phone?
Your data lives on your device. It moves with a full iPhone backup and restore (iCloud or computer), but deleting the app deletes it.
