What the App is

Blindspot maps the locations of automated license plate readers and similar surveillance devices, warns you when you are approaching one, and can suggest driving routes that pass fewer of them. It is intended to help people understand where they are being recorded in public.

The data is not guaranteed

Camera locations come from OpenStreetMap, a public database contributed to and verified by volunteers, and are used under the Open Database License. That data is frequently incomplete and sometimes wrong.

You should assume the App does not know about every camera. A location may be missing, out of date, moved, removed, or simply mistaken. The direction and coverage area shown for a device is an estimate. Nothing in the App should be read as a claim that an area is unmonitored — the absence of a camera on the map means only that no one has recorded one there.
